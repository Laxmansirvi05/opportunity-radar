# UI/UX Brief – Opportunity Radar

## Design Philosophy

Opportunity Radar follows a student-first SaaS design approach focused on simplicity, trust, and productivity. The platform is designed to help students discover, evaluate, track, and contribute career opportunities without information overload.

Core principles:

* Minimal learning curve
* Fast opportunity discovery
* Clear visual hierarchy
* Trust and transparency
* Mobile-friendly responsive experience
* Consistent design system across all modules

## Design System

### Color Palette

* Primary Blue: Used for primary actions and navigation
* Success Green: Verification and approval states
* Warning Orange: Deadlines and attention-required actions
* Error Red: Rejected submissions and critical alerts
* Neutral Gray Scale: Layout structure and content hierarchy

### Typography

* Modern sans-serif font
* Large page titles
* Medium section headings
* High readability body text
* Consistent spacing and line heights

## User Experience Goals

Students should be able to:

1. Discover opportunities quickly
2. Evaluate opportunity quality
3. Save and organize opportunities
4. Track applications
5. Receive deadline reminders
6. Contribute new opportunities
7. Report incorrect listings

## Screen Inventory

### Landing Page

Purpose:
Introduce Opportunity Radar and communicate platform value.

### Authentication

Purpose:
Simple onboarding using Google Sign-In.

### Student Dashboard

Purpose:
Central workspace for opportunity management.

### Opportunity Hub

Purpose:
Discover internships, scholarships, jobs, workshops, and fellowships.

### Search Experience

Purpose:
Advanced filtering and opportunity exploration.

### Opportunity Detail

Purpose:
Provide complete information for decision making.

### Application Tracker

Purpose:
Track application progress from discovery to outcome.

### Notifications Center

Purpose:
Deadline alerts, approval updates, and reminders.

### Company Intelligence

Purpose:
Help students research organizations before applying.

### User Profile Settings

Purpose:
Manage personal information and preferences.

### Submit Opportunity

Purpose:
Enable community-driven opportunity contributions.

### Report Broken Opportunity

Purpose:
Maintain platform data quality through community reporting.

### Moderation Center

Purpose:
Review and verify submitted opportunities.

### Trust & Platform Operations Dashboard

Purpose:
Monitor platform integrity, verification quality, and contributor health.

## Accessibility

* Keyboard navigable interface
* Accessible color contrast
* Clear button states
* Consistent navigation patterns
* Responsive layouts for multiple screen sizes

## Expected Outcome

The final experience should feel similar to a modern SaaS platform while remaining approachable for college students and fresh graduates.
